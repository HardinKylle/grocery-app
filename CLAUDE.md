# Grocery App

Private grocery Inventory and Shopping List PWA with barcode scanning. Domain terms are in `GLOSSARY.md`; decisions in `docs/adr/`.

## Commits

- Conventional Commits, short subject (≤50 chars), body only when the why isn't obvious.
- Never add a `Co-Authored-By` trailer.
